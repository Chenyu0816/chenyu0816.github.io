I study the carbon storage, cooling and other ecosystem services of urban trees and parks, combining municipal tree inventories, i-Tree Eco, GIS and remote sensing to support the management of urban green space.我研究城市树木与公园的碳储存、降温等生态系统服务，结合市政树木清单、i-Tree Eco、GIS 与遥感，为城市绿地的管理提供依据。

A designer first设计出身

I came to ecology through drawing. Environmental design and landscape architecture taught me to read a site before measuring it; that habit still shapes how I map trees, carbon and heat.我是从画图走进生态学的。环境设计和景观建筑的训练让我先读懂场地，再去测量它，这个习惯至今影响着我如何去表达树木、碳与热。
